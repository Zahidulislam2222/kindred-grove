# US/EU applicability and implementation register

**Research checked: 2026-09-24** · **Status: Phase 2 development controls evidenced; commercial applicability and launch controls remain open**

This is an engineering and operating register for the Kindred Grove Shopify theme. It records source-backed questions, controls, evidence, owners, and next gates. It is not a jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction legal opinion and does not decide that a law applies to this merchant. Applicable law depends on the merchant’s legal entity, locations, customers, products, sales/data volumes, processing purposes, vendors, and fulfillment model; those facts have not been supplied as a verified commercial profile.

The demo is not a live-sale authorization. Phase 3 implementation is a separate milestone and is not included in the Phase 2 work described here. No paid legal, tax, accessibility, or security review has been commissioned.

## How to use this register

A requirement can be **verified at theme layer** while its legal applicability or the platform-wide process remains **open**. “Verified” means the described code/configuration behavior has the named evidence; it does not mean the merchant is compliant. An unknown merchant fact is a research task, not a negative applicability finding.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Verified at theme layer | The identified theme behavior passed the cited source, automated, or real-browser check. Platform/app behavior and legal sufficiency are not implied. |
| Partially verified | Some technical behavior is known, but a material platform, vendor, route, or operating check remains open. |
| Merchant decision required | The owner must supply business facts, approve a policy, or operate the process. |
| Research required | Current jurisdiction/product/vendor analysis is still needed. |
| Conditional trigger | Reassess only if the named product, audience, market, vendor, or processing is introduced. |

### Accountable roles

- **Merchant/operator:** supplies true business facts; decides audience, products, markets, fulfilment, retention, notices, response channels, vendors, and who handles requests/incidents. This role cannot be replaced by a theme developer.
- **Project owner / technical reviewer:** maintains the source-to-control map, reviews Shopify configuration and vendor evidence with the merchant, commissions any separately authorized qualified advice, and blocks unreviewed releases.
- **Theme developer:** implements only the approved requirements, keeps data minimization and consent gates, and produces reproducible test evidence. The developer does not decide legal applicability or product claims.
- **Privacy, legal, tax, food-label, or accessibility specialist:** consulted by the merchant when facts or law require a professional interpretation. No such engagement is represented as completed.

## Phase 2 evidence baseline

The reviewed development artifact passed the integrated browser suite with **41 passed, 0 failed, 5 documented skipped, 0 flaky**; the local security/configuration suite passed **95/95**; selected local, frozen, and downloaded theme files matched **150/150 SHA-256 hashes**. Separate guarded tests of actual quiz and wholesale alternate templates/flows passed **2/2**. Quiz results linked to a rendered HTTP 200 collection containing products; the five answers remained in memory, were absent from local and session storage, and reset on reload. Active quiz and wholesale WCAG 2.2 A/AA axe scans passed **2/2** with zero violations; the quiz progressbar was visible and had an accessible name.

The five integrated-suite skips were evidenced: a single-variant product; canonical Quiz route (404); canonical Wholesale route (404); unavailable test article; and account route redirecting to a Shopify-hosted surface. Testing an alternate template on the existing Contact resource did not create canonical page resources. Shopify Network Intelligence remained enabled. Hosted checkout/accounts, installed apps and pixels, and other platform services were not declared under theme control. The source evidence and limits are summarized in the [Phase 2 acceptance record](../BUILD-PLAN.md#phase-2--safe-functional-demo-storefront) and [theme privacy inventory](PRIVACY.md).

## Launch sequence and evidence to retain

1. **Merchant facts:** legal entity and locations; intended states/countries; B2C/B2B model; products, labels, suppliers/importers; audiences; fulfillment; sales/data-volume facts needed for thresholds; actual vendors and data purposes. Store these decisions in a private business record; do not invent them in public docs.
2. **Data-flow and vendor map:** for each page/form/script/Shopify feature, record field/event, purpose, legal role, consent/basis, recipient, subprocessors, region/transfer, retention, deletion route, contract, and owner. Recheck network behavior from a fresh context after every app/pixel change.
3. **Notices and visitor controls:** write merchant-specific privacy, cookie, contact, shipping, returns, food, and accessibility content; link notices where collection starts; verify translated variants, native consent regions, rejection/partial/withdrawal, GPC, and rights channels. Theme controls are one input to this review.
4. **Rights and retention rehearsal:** with synthetic test data, exercise a request through Shopify and each actual vendor. Test identity handling, timers, search/export/correction/deletion, exceptions, backups, email suppression, confirmation, and evidence retention. Do not use real customer data in test scripts.
5. **Product/market approval:** substantiate label and advertising claims; configure tax, shipping, returns, accessibility disclosures, and checkout for each approved market; test total prices and fulfillment cases. Keep a dated review record.
6. **Release gate:** close all triggered `research required` and `merchant decision required` entries; re-run local and real-flow checks against the exact frozen artifact; record independent review and parity hashes. A source test, policy draft, or successful preview is not a substitute for the missing business decision.

For each requirement, retain `ID → checked source/date → trigger and facts → decision/owner → code or configuration control → test/evidence reference → remaining issue → next review`. Store credentials, customer information, incident details, and private merchant facts only in approved private records, never this public register.

## Review cadence

Review this register at least quarterly once the store is operating, before each launch or market expansion, and whenever products, audience, vendor, purpose, Shopify settings, legal rules, or data flow changes. Recheck dated law/application milestones shortly before they take effect. The first formal applicability review is due **before any commercial launch**; the Phase 2 technical evidence above does not close that gate.
